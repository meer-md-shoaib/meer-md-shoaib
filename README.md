# 💫 About Me:
I'm **Meer Mohammed Shoaib**, a Computer Science Engineering student who enjoys building things that are smarter than me... at least until they throw an error. My interests revolve around **Full Stack Development, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity**, and I've had the opportunity to explore them through **Python Development** and **Cybersecurity internships**, personal projects, and leading technical events at my university. Whether it's creating an AI learning platform, a real-time chat application, or securing vulnerable web applications, I love turning ideas into reality, one commit at a time.<br><br>When I'm not convincing my code to compile, you'll probably find me watching movies, building websites just because I had a "cool idea" at 2 AM, exploring the latest technologies, or cheering for twenty cars driving at impossible speeds in **Formula 1**.
